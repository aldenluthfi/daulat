## Behavior decisions

- **No new `BattleState` field (user philosophy, same as F5's no-`MapState`
  field).** The plan is battle-lifetime transient state, so it lives in a
  file-scope `static AIPlan AI_PLAN` in `ai.c`, like the ~20 battle statics in
  `battle.c`. `BattleState` and the save format are byte-for-byte unchanged;
  the plan is never serialized. `ai_reset()` (called at `battle_begin`) clears
  it so a plan from a previous battle can never replay.
- **Execution is replay, not recompute.** `ai_take_turn` builds the plan once
  (or reuses the one Divination already stored), then replays each stored
  buy/move via `battle_buy`/`battle_move`, emitting the unchanged `log ai buy`
  / `log ai move` lines. On an unchanged board this makes the revealed plan
  equal the execution by construction.
- **Deterministic fingerprint invalidation.** The plan stores an occupancy-only
  hash (piece id + side per cell; never cp/actions/meter, which change at
  `turn_start`). `ai_ensure_plan` rebuilds and emits
  `log ai replan reason=board-changed` when the live fingerprint differs. A
  stored action that is illegal at replay (an intra-turn board mutation the
  build did not model) emits `log ai replan reason=illegal` and rebuilds from
  the current board (bounded retry). Both paths keep replay robust.
- **Build uses reversible board patches + phantom pieces, per Q3
  (`audit-plan-v2.md:1822`).** `ai_plan_build` mirrors the live greedy loop
  (buy a knight adjacent to the king while affordable, else advance the piece
  whose best move most reduces Chebyshev distance to the enemy king). Moves are
  simulated by repointing the two board cells and the piece square, recorded
  for revert; buys are simulated with `battle_spawn` (effect-light off the
  human seat) so the bought knight blocks and can itself be advanced in-turn.
  Every patch is reverted and every phantom freed before return, so the real
  board is unchanged afterward. Next-turn resources are predicted as
  `cp + 10` (turn_start's base income) and `actions = 3`; income-modifying and
  buy-cost effects are not modelled at build time — replay revalidation
  self-corrects (documented, consistent with F5's pragmatic deviation).
- **Card reveal is authoritatively empty (recorded user decision).** The enemy
  has no card mechanics at all: `battle_project_hand` and `battle_realize_hand`
  both early-return for `side != HUMAN_SIDE`, so the AI never draws, holds, or
  autosells a hand. Divination therefore reveals no cards because the enemy has
  none. Giving the AI a hand is new AI-card infrastructure and an AI-economy
  change, which `res/audit-plan-v2.md:471` puts outside the F6 gate. The
  `log ai plan card … fate=autosell` emitter is present but emits nothing while
  the enemy hand stays empty. Gap recorded below for a later AI-card phase.

### 1. Gate — unchanged board (play Divination, then end)

```text
play i=2
  -> log ai plan side=1 count=3
     log ai plan buy piece=35 x=5 y=0
     log ai plan move fx=5 fy=0 tx=4 ty=2
     log ai plan move fx=4 fy=2 tx=3 ty=4
     log divination
     log effect name="Divination" trigger=ON_CARD_PLAY
end
  -> log ai buy piece=Knight x=5 y=0
     log ai move fx=5 fy=0 tx=4 ty=2
     log ai move fx=4 fy=2 tx=3 ty=4
```

Revealed plan == execution, in order: buy Knight (id 35) at (5,0); move the
just-bought knight (5,0)->(4,2)->(3,4). The phantom buy is correctly advanced
in the same simulated turn, matching the live greedy. Card lines: none (enemy
holds no cards).

### 2. Invalidation (Divination, change board, end)

```text
play i=2                     -> same plan revealed as case 1
move fx=6 fy=11 tx=6 ty=10   (white king moved -> occupancy changes)
end
  -> log ai replan reason=board-changed
     log ai buy piece=Knight x=5 y=0
     log ai move fx=5 fy=0 tx=4 ty=2
     log ai move fx=4 fy=2 tx=3 ty=4
```

The fingerprint mismatch announces `reason=board-changed` before execution,
then the AI rebuilds from the current board. (Black's first two actions happen
to coincide because the moved white king is far from the advancing knight; the
reason line is the decisive invalidation evidence.)

- After `play i=2`, `hand` shows Divination removed (slots Reforge, Formation
  remain) — the card was consumed by the play.
