## Behavior decisions

- **Tick rule (user decision).** Rather than add an `UNTIL_OWNER_TURN`
  duration or a `BattleState` crossing field (both rejected), a side's
  `TURNS_n` effects now tick at the start of that side's own next turn.
  `TURNS_n` therefore lasts n full rounds. This is one lifecycle-rule fix
  that repairs three GDD defects at once: Counter Coup (`GDD:281`), Last
  Stand (`GDD:276`), and Longwei Climax's damage-taken reducer — all
  previously expired before the opponent's attack. Own-half offensive
  buffs are unaffected (damage-dealt and cost queries fire only for the
  acting side during its own half).
- **Cross-list compensation (user decision).** The relocation shortens
  debuffs a caster attaches onto the enemy list by one enemy turn, so
  Mingzhu's Seal, Palace Decree, and Citadel are each bumped by one to
  preserve their GDD durations. The duration doc comment now defines the
  semantics precisely.
- **Vorath is a capacity increase.** `battle_meter_max` folds the piece
  sum through `QUERY_METER_AMOUNT`; the capacity effect adds 20 per two
  losses to that maximum before the initial meter, refill, and 200%
  ceiling all read it. The post-meter `battle_meter_gain` pressure path is
  gone (`GDD:659-664`).
- **Timur uses one stable event.** A damage-and-cascade transaction fires
  exactly one `ON_CASCADE_END` for the receiver carrying the lowest meter
  reached; the watcher swaps when `battle_cascade_origin() >= 20` and that
  lowest is `< 20`, independent of any flipped piece's side. Nested
  Counter-Coup-echo transactions fire for the other side with a saved
  origin register, so re-entrancy does not mix crossings.
- **Bulk Discount true-up (user decision).** Total per-turn discount always
  equals the single cheapest committed cost. Each buy is adjusted by
  `granted - target`, so a later cheaper buy repays the earlier
  over-discount and becomes the free piece. Only committed buys count
  (`ON_PIECE_BUY`), excluding failed or previewed buys. Supersedes the
  plan's turn-end-refund wording and v1 item H13.
- **Shared Royal Substitution pool.** Manual innate swaps and Timur's
  automatic swap draw from one `eff_noop` mark; the limit is 1, or 2 at
  Zarqan mastery 3 (`GDD:972`, `:980`). `QUERY_PIECE_MOVES` only gates
  (never counts); a use is spent on `ON_PIECE_MOVE` when the friendly king
  is left on the mover's origin square (a real swap).
- **Double Time pricing.** Home Kewarani buys lose the 40% discount
  (`x*100/60`); foreign Kewarani buys pay 40% (`x*140/120`); Selassie
  mastery 3 declines the foreign effect, leaving `battle_price`'s 20%
  (`GDD:236`, `:979`).
- **Flagged — Shahzadeh interaction.** Shahzadeh is a Zarqan piece that
  swaps with the king through its own movegen. Under uniform counting its
  king-swap also spends a Royal Substitution use, consistent with GDD's
  "any Zarqan piece swaps with the king." If the Shahzadeh swap should be
  unlimited and independent of the innate limit, that is a one-line
  exclusion — surfaced for the user's call, not assumed.
- **Note — `vorath_attach_capacity`.** `battle_begin` names this one data
  hook; all Vorath identity logic lives in `universal.c`. This strictly
  reduces the item naming that the deleted in-engine `eff_vorath_pressure`
  carried. Folding it into a generic pre-meter run hook is a candidate for
  the S-phase, not required for F1.

## Runtime evidence

All fixtures use an isolated temporary profile: `new` then `save`, patch
only legal persistent fields, `load`, then drive public commands.

### Vorath capacity (route: baseline B3)

Setup: `new seed=2 difficulty=0 challenge=7`, patch `vorath=2`.

```text
load / enter id=0 / select i=0
log effect name="Vorath Pressure" trigger=QUERY_METER_AMOUNT
state side=white turn=1 cp=30 meter=10 enemy_meter=30 actions=3
```

Probes: `vorath=0` -> `enemy_meter=10` (no fire); `vorath=4` ->
`enemy_meter=50`. Previously the 200% clamp cut the lone-King case to 20.

### Timur crossing (route: baseline B7)

Setup: `seed=1`, `battles=1` (human black), all pieces unlocked, Timur's
Conquest in opening hand. Play Timur, buy Wazirs to a meter of 40, drive
AI attacks.

```text
log damage side=black amount=30            (meter 40 -> 10, crosses 20)
log effect name="Timur's Conquest" trigger=ON_CASCADE_END
row y=0 cells=····N·k·····   (pre:  king at 6,0)
row y=1 cells=···wwww·····
row y=0 cells=····N·w·····   (post: Wazir at 6,0)
row y=1 cells=···kwww·····   (post: king swapped to 3,1)
```

Probes: no fire when the hit lands at exactly meter 20 (not below); later
crossings in the same battle do not fire again at mastery 0 (limit 1).

### Counter Coup lifetime (route: baseline B8)

Setup: `seed=7 difficulty=3`, `battles=1` (human black), cards bit 8 only so
Counter Coup fills every draw. Replay `play i=0` then `end`.

```text
log damage side=white amount=15
log effect name="Counter Coup" trigger=QUERY_METER_DAMAGE_TAKEN
log damage side=black amount=30
```

The 30 the human takes echoes 15 (50%) to the enemy. Human white
(`battles=0 seed=89`) echoes 5 of a 10 hit. Pre-fix (Phase 0 B8) the echo
was absent — the `TURNS_1` copy expired at the playing side's half-turn end.

### Bulk Discount (route: baseline)

Setup: relic bit 3 set, Longwei battle. Bing costs 3, piece 20 costs 12.

```text
three Bings:      cp 30 -> 27 -> 24 -> 24   (3rd free; cheapest 3)
12,12,3 order:    cp 30 ------> 6 --> 6      (cheapest 3 free)
3,12,12 order:    cp 30 -> 27 -> 15 -> 6     (3rd pays 9 = 12 - 3)
log effect name="Bulk Discount" trigger=QUERY_PIECE_CP_COST_BUY
```

Probe: two buys give no discount. Total discount always equals the single
cheapest committed cost.

### Double Time pricing (route: baseline)

Setup: Kewarani mastery 1, then 3.

```text
Medeq (val 10) home, Kewarani:      paid 10  (no 40% home discount)
Medeq foreign, Longwei, mastery 1:  paid 7   (140%, halved by node modifier)
Medeq foreign, Longwei, mastery 3:  paid 6   (120%; DT foreign declines)
```

Ratio 7:6 = 140:120 isolates the Double Time markup from the node modifier.

### Royal Substitution limit (route: baseline)

Setup: Zarqan mastery 1, then 3. Buy Wazir beside king, inspect `moves`,
swap via `move`, re-inspect.

```text
mastery 1: 1st Wazir moves offer king square (6,11); after swap the 2nd
           Wazir moves omit it (no Royal Substitution fire) — limit 1.
mastery 3: two swaps counted (ON_PIECE_MOVE x2); 3rd piece moves omit the
           king square — limit 2.
```

Probe: repeated `moves` queries before a swap never decrement the pool;
only the executed `ON_PIECE_MOVE` swap counts.
